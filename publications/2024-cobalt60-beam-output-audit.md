# Clinical audit of beam output of Cobalt-60 teletherapy unit at AECH-BINOR

Khan Z, Ghani MU, **Mujtaba T**, Rehman SU, Khan KU, Maaz M, Muhammad AN

*Open Access Library Journal*, 11(4), 1–7 (2024) · [doi:10.4236/oalib.1111446](https://doi.org/10.4236/oalib.1111446)

**Keywords:** radiotherapy · dosimetry · Cobalt-60 · IAEA TRS-398 · clinical audit

## Summary
A nine-year (2014–2022) audit of beam-output dosimetry for the Cobalt-60 teletherapy unit at BINOR cancer hospital, following the IAEA TRS-398 protocol.

- The mean deviation between measured and calculated dose rates was **1.904 ± 1.41**.
- This is below the IAEA guideline of ±2 %, confirming that the hospital's dosimetry was consistent with international standards throughout the audit period.
