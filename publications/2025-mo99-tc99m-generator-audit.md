# Assessment of molybdenum-99m content in 99Mo/Tc-99m generator: A clinical audit at Cancer Hospital

Ghani MU, **Mujtaba T**, Khan KU, Rehman SU, Rauf M, Rehman A

*Pakistan Journal of Nuclear Medicine*, 14(1) (2025) · [Article page](https://www.pjnmed.com/?mno=195911)

**Keywords:** nuclear medicine · radiopharmacy · quality control · Tc-99m generator · clinical audit

## Summary
A clinical audit of molybdenum content in the technetium-99m eluate from ⁹⁹Mo/⁹⁹ᵐTc generators used at a cancer hospital. Checking molybdenum breakthrough is a routine radiopharmacy quality-control step: it confirms that each eluate stays within permitted limits before Tc-99m radiopharmaceuticals are prepared for patients.
