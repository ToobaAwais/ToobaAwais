# Evaluation of parameters affecting intrinsic uniformity to perform best optimum daily QC

Ahmed A, Rehman A, Ghani HMU, **Mujtaba T**, Khan NU, Khan KU, Rauf M

*Journal of Chemotherapy and Cancer Research*, 2(2), 1–4 (2024) · [doi:10.61440/JCCR.2024.v2.11](https://doi.org/10.61440/JCCR.2024.v2.11)

**Keywords:** nuclear medicine · gamma camera · quality control · intrinsic uniformity · SPECT

## Summary
Intrinsic uniformity is the core daily quality-control test for a gamma camera. This study tested how acquisition parameters affect the measured uniformity on both detectors of a dual-head GE Infinia system, to define a reliable benchmark protocol for daily QC.

- Parameters varied: Tc-99m point-source activity (~100–700 µCi), source-to-detector distance (90–290 cm) and total image counts (30–70 million).
- Uniformity improved with higher activity, more counts and greater distance; short distances gave markedly poorer uniformity.
- Recommended daily QC settings: 140 keV with a 20 % energy window, 256 × 256 matrix, about 500 µCi source activity, 60 million counts and a source distance greater than 2 m.
