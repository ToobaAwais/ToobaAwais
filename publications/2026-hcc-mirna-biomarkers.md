# Integrative analysis of tissue and circulating miRNAs as biomarkers for progression and survival in hepatocellular carcinoma

Jabri A, Mhannayeh A, Alsharif M, Taftafa B, **Mujtaba T**, Akbar A, Abu-zaid A, Mir TA, Khan MI, Ahmed F, Arora I, Yaqinuddin A

*Non-coding RNA Research*, 16, 167–177 (2026) · [doi:10.1016/j.ncrna.2025.11.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ncrna.2025.11.002)

**Keywords:** miRNA · hepatocellular carcinoma · TCGA-LIHC · GEO · survival analysis · biomarkers

## Summary
Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) is usually diagnosed late, and few biomarkers are available for risk stratification. This study combined tumour miRNA sequencing data from TCGA-LIHC with serum miRNA profiles from GEO (GSE113740) to find miRNAs linked to disease progression and patient outcome.

- **16 miRNAs** were significantly dysregulated in HCC tissue. miR-187 and miR-6718 were associated with poorer survival, while miR-5589 showed a protective effect.
- miR-106b rose with tumour stage, and the miR-124 family was reduced in metastatic tumours.
- Enrichment analysis pointed to PI3K-Akt, MAPK signalling and nucleocytoplasmic transport.
- In serum, miR-3619-3p, miR-1290 and miR-1185-2-3p tracked AFP levels and disease stage, supporting their potential as non-invasive markers.
